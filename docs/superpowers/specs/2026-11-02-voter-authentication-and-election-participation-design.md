# Pollwise — Voter Authentication and Election Participation Design Document

**Document type:** System Design Specification  
**System:** Pollwise Institutional Voting Platform  
**Scope:** Voter authentication, ballot experience, personal-device voting, supervised multi-PC voting centres, and independent election voter rolls  
**Target File:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-voter-authentication-and-election-participation-design.md`  
**Date:** 2026-11-02  

---

## 1. Intent and Why

The Pollwise Institutional Voting Platform provides secure, verifiable, secret-ballot elections for universities, colleges, student unions, and professional organizations.

Institutions require a voting experience that is:
1. **Low friction yet tamper-resistant:** Voters authenticate using their familiar institutional identifier (Institutional Email or Institutional ID) and verify access with an instant six-digit on-screen OTP generated by Pollwise (not delivered via email or SMS).
2. **Accessible across personal phones and supervised kiosks:** The exact same eligibility rules, authentication, ballot service, participation ledger, and duplicate-voting enforcement apply whether voting on a private smartphone or an on-campus multi-PC voting centre terminal.
3. **Structured for clear voter intent:** Ballots are presented candidate-by-candidate (one position per screen) with explicit support for candidate selection, optional position skipping, and full review before final submission.
4. **Strictly private (Secret Ballot):** The system records *that* a voter participated to prevent duplicate voting, but enforces cryptographic and architectural separation so that ballot selections can never be linked to voter identity by administrators, supervisors, or system logs.
5. **Decoupled from general platform user accounts:** Voter rolls are independent per election. Voters do not need pre-existing Pollwise user accounts or passwords to vote.

## 2. Goals, Non-Goals, and Anti-Goals

### Goals
- Enable election administrators to configure voter authentication using either `institutional_email` or `institutional_id`.
- Support independent voter rolls per election with CSV/manual imports, validation previews, and duplicate detection.
- Provide on-screen 6-digit OTP generation and verification directly in the voter's browser without external SMS/email dependencies.
- Deliver a responsive single-position-per-screen ballot wizard supporting candidate selection, optional position skipping, review, and confirmation.
- Guarantee atomic ballot submission and enforce strict duplicate-voting prevention: exactly one accepted ballot per eligible voter record per election (`UNIQUE(election_id, voter_record_id)`).
- Support supervised multi-PC voting centres with registered terminals, automated reset on completion/abandonment, and supervisor monitoring.
- Maintain total separation of identity and vote content across the isolated database architecture.

### Non-Goals
- Institutional account onboarding and platform-level super admin functions (outside this voter participation scope).
- External delivery of verification codes via SMTP, SES, Twilio, or SMS (the specification strictly requires on-screen OTP display).
- Ranked-choice, approval, or multi-winner voting methods (the scope specifies single-choice per position with optional skip).
- Automatic creation of fake user accounts for imported voter roll entries.

### Anti-Goals
- **No Early Participation Flags:** Never mark a voter as having voted merely because they entered their identifier, viewed the OTP, or created a session. Mark participation as `submitted` only upon successful ballot acceptance.
- **No Joint Ballot-Identity Tables:** Never store voter record IDs, institutional emails/IDs, or session tokens in `ballot_db` or ballot records.
- **No Fabricated or Mocked Data:** Never create fake candidates, positions, or voters to make interfaces look populated.
- **No Client Authority:** Never allow the frontend or client to dictate eligibility, participation state, or ballot ownership.

## 3. Approaches Considered

### Approach 1: Election-Domain Architecture with Decoupled Secret Ballot Box (Selected)
* **Architecture:**
  * `election_db`: Hosts election configuration (`election_voter_auth_config`), independent voter rolls (`election_voter_records`), challenge records (`voter_otp_challenges`), voting sessions (`voting_sessions`), participation ledger (`election_participation`), and terminal infrastructure (`voting_centres`, `voting_terminals`).
  * `ballot_db`: Hosts strictly anonymous `ballots` and choices. Completely free of voter identifiers, session tokens, or correlation metadata.
  * `identity_db`: Remains independent. `election_voter_records.voter_reference_id` or optional `user_id` can reference it as a nullable foreign key for optional account linking, but voting never requires a user account.
* **Migration Strategy:**
  * Replaces the legacy account-based blind-credential voting mechanism (`identity_db.election_eligibility`, `POST /voting-credential`, `ballot_credential_redemptions`).
  * Upgrades `VotingBoothPage.tsx` into the new unified voter experience supporting mobile and multi-PC kiosks.
* **Tradeoffs & Evaluation:**
  * *Pros:* Clear domain ownership. Since voter rolls are election-specific and independent of platform users, placing them in `election_db` eliminates cross-database lookups during voter authentication. `ballot_db` remains completely blind and tamper-evident.
  * *Cons:* Requires a coordinated atomic submission across `ballot_db` (store ballot) and `election_db` (record participation) using an idempotency key.
* **Status:** Selected by project stakeholders.

### Approach 2: Identity-Domain Voter Registry with Cross-Database Coordination
* **Architecture:**
  * `identity_db`: Hosts voter records, OTP challenges, and participation states.
  * `election_db`: Hosts elections, auth configs, positions, and candidates.
  * `ballot_db`: Hosts ballots.
* **Tradeoffs & Evaluation:**
  * *Pros:* Reuses some existing identity database models.
  * *Cons:* Heavily couples independent voter lists to user accounts, contrary to user directives. Incurs distributed cross-database queries on every authentication step.
* **Status:** Rejected.

## 4. Database Models and Invariants

All new models belong to `app.db.base.ElectionBase` (targeting `election_db`), with `ballots` in `app.db.base.BallotBase` (targeting `ballot_db`).

### 4.1 `election_voter_auth_config` (election_db)
Configured per election:
- `id: UUID (PK)`
- `election_id: UUID (FK elections.id, UNIQUE, ON DELETE CASCADE)`
- `identifier_type: str` ('institutional_email' | 'institutional_id')
- `identifier_label: str` (e.g., "Student ID", "Staff Number", default "Institutional Email" / "Institutional ID")
- `identifier_normalization_rules: dict (JSON)` (trim, case-insensitive, regex pattern)
- `otp_length: int` (fixed at 6)
- `otp_expiry_seconds: int` (default 300, 5 mins)
- `otp_max_attempts: int` (default 5)
- `otp_regeneration_cooldown_seconds: int` (default 30)
- `session_idle_timeout_seconds: int` (default 600, 10 mins)
- `session_max_duration_seconds: int` (default 3600, 1 hour)
- `allow_session_resume: bool` (default True)
- `allow_position_skip: bool` (default True)
- `configuration_locked_at: datetime | None`
- `created_at, updated_at: datetime`

### 4.2 `election_voter_records` (election_db)
Independent approved voter roll:
- `id: UUID (PK)`
- `election_id: UUID (FK elections.id, index, ON DELETE CASCADE)`
- `identifier_type: str` ('institutional_email' | 'institutional_id')
- `normalized_identifier: str (index)` (e.g. trimmed lowercase email or normalized ID)
- `voter_reference_id: UUID | None (index)` (optional link to users.id)
- `eligibility_status: str` ('eligible', 'ineligible', 'suspended', 'revoked', 'pending_review', default 'eligible')
- `voter_group: str | None` (e.g., department, year, constituency)
- `source: str` ('csv_import' | 'manual_entry')
- `created_at, updated_at: datetime`
- **Constraints:** `UNIQUE(election_id, identifier_type, normalized_identifier)`

### 4.3 `voter_otp_challenges` (election_db)
Ephemeral on-screen challenges:
- `id: UUID (PK)`
- `election_id: UUID (FK elections.id, index)`
- `voter_record_id: UUID (FK election_voter_records.id, index)`
- `challenge_hash: str (index, unique)` (secure token identifying challenge)
- `otp_hash: str` (SHA-256 hash of 6-digit OTP + salt; never stored in plaintext)
- `attempt_count: int` (default 0)
- `expires_at: datetime`
- `consumed_at: datetime | None`
- `created_at: datetime`

### 4.4 `voting_sessions` (election_db)
Active voter sessions:
- `id: UUID (PK)`
- `election_id: UUID (FK elections.id, index)`
- `voter_record_id: UUID (FK election_voter_records.id, index)`
- `terminal_id: UUID | None (index)`
- `session_token_hash: str (index, unique)`
- `session_status: str` ('active', 'idle', 'submitting', 'submitted', 'expired', 'cancelled', 'invalidated', default 'active')
- `draft_payload: dict (JSON) | None` (encrypted/obfuscated temporary client draft during session)
- `idempotency_key: str | None (index)`
- `confirmation_reference: str | None`
- `expires_at: datetime`
- `last_activity_at: datetime`
- `created_at: datetime`

### 4.5 `election_participation` (election_db)
The permanent participation ledger:
- `id: UUID (PK)`
- `election_id: UUID (FK elections.id, index)`
- `voter_record_id: UUID (FK election_voter_records.id, index)`
- `participation_status: str` (default 'submitted')
- `submitted_at: datetime`
- `confirmation_reference: str (unique)` (e.g., "PW-ELEC-XXXXXX")
- **Hard Database Invariant:** `UNIQUE(election_id, voter_record_id)`

### 4.6 `ballots` (ballot_db)
Strict secret ballot:
- `id: UUID (PK)`
- `election_id: UUID (index)`
- `configuration_hash: str`
- `ballot_payload: dict (JSON)` (array of position choices: candidate ID or 'skipped')
- `ballot_status: str` ('accepted')
- `accepted_at: datetime`
- **Architectural Invariant:** Absolutely no foreign key or column for `voter_record_id`, `user_id`, `session_id`, or `terminal_id`.

### 4.7 `voting_centres` and `voting_terminals` (election_db)
Multi-PC voting centre management:
- `voting_centres`: `id`, `institution_id`, `name`, `location_description`, `status` ('active', 'inactive'), timestamps.
- `voting_terminals`: `id`, `voting_centre_id`, `institution_id`, `terminal_name`, `authorized_election_id`, `terminal_status` ('idle', 'identifier_entry', 'challenge_verification', 'voting', 'review', 'submitting', 'resetting', 'offline', 'disabled'), `auth_token_hash`, `last_seen_at`, timestamps.

## 5. Voter Roll Import & Validation (CSV & Manual)

1. **Upload & Parsing:**
   - Admin uploads CSV or enters records manually in the election management view.
   - Headers supported: `identifier`, `voter_group`, `eligibility_status` (optional).
2. **Validation Engine:**
   - Validates each row against election `identifier_type`:
     - If `institutional_email`: RFC 5322 syntax check, lowercase normalization, whitespace trimming.
     - If `institutional_id`: Whitespace trimming, length & allowed character checks.
   - Detects within-file duplicates and conflicts with existing registered voter records in that election.
3. **Preview & Confirmation Report:**
   - `POST /api/elections/{id}/voters/preview` returns a structured preview: `valid_count`, `duplicate_count`, `invalid_count`, list of error rows with specific issues, and a temporary `preview_batch_id` (or direct validated multipart confirmation).
   - `POST /api/elections/{id}/voters/import` commits the records in chunks directly into `election_voter_records` without requiring or generating user accounts.
   - For high volume rosters (10k+ rows), batch streaming ensures low memory consumption.

## 6. Voter Authentication & On-Screen OTP Flow

1. **Landing & Identifier Entry (`/elections/:id/vote`):**
   - Displays election details, institution name, status, and voting instructions.
   - If election is open, presents a single input matching the configured identifier:
     - `Institutional Email` or `Institutional ID` (with custom label, e.g. "Student ID").
2. **Identifier Submission (`POST /api/elections/{id}/voter-auth/request-challenge`):**
   - Backend checks:
     1. Election exists and status is `open` (within `opens_at` and `closes_at`).
     2. Identifier normalized.
     3. Voter record exists in `election_voter_records` with status `eligible`.
     4. Voter has not already submitted (`election_participation` check).
     5. Cooldown period checked if regenerating.
   - Generates cryptographically secure 6-digit numeric OTP (e.g. `secrets.choice`).
   - Stores SHA-256 hash in `voter_otp_challenges`.
   - Returns `challenge_id`, `expires_in_seconds`, and `otp_code` in the JSON response so the client screen displays it immediately.
   - **No external delivery:** Never sends via email or SMS.
3. **On-Screen Display & Verification (`POST /api/elections/{id}/voter-auth/verify-challenge`):**
   - Screen shows large formatted 6-digit OTP (e.g. `4 8 2 9 1 6`) with expiry countdown.
   - Voter enters the 6-digit code into input boxes.
   - Backend verifies hash, checks expiration, increments attempts (locks if > 5), marks challenge consumed.
   - Creates authenticated `voting_sessions` record and returns a session token.

## 7. Candidate-by-Candidate Ballot Wizard

1. **Instructions Screen:**
   - Explains voting rules, number of positions, skip rules, and secret ballot policy.
2. **Position-per-Screen Progression:**
   - Display: `Position X of Y`, Position title, description, approved candidate list (names, photos, manifestos).
   - Candidate selection: Clicking a candidate marks them selected (single-choice).
   - Skip option: Prominent **"Skip this Position"** button (enabled if position is optional).
   - Required positions: If configured as required, skip is disabled with clear explanation.
   - Navigation: **"Previous"** and **"Next"** buttons. Selections persist in session state/draft.
3. **Review Screen:**
   - Displays all positions with their current selection: Candidate Name or *"Skipped"*.
   - Allows jumping back to any position to modify choice before submission.
4. **Submission Confirmation:**
   - Confirmation modal: *"Submit your ballot? After your ballot is submitted, you cannot change your selections."*
   - Voter confirms to proceed.

## 8. Atomic Ballot Submission & Duplicate-Voting Prevention

1. **Submission Request (`POST /api/elections/{id}/ballot/submit`):**
   - Request includes `session_token`, ballot selections (`[{position_id, candidate_id | null, skipped: bool}]`), and `idempotency_key`.
2. **Atomic Verification & Two-Database Coordination Protocol:**
   - Step A (`election_db`): Authenticate session token, verify active/unexpired status, verify election is open, and check that `voter_record_id` has not already submitted in `election_participation`.
   - Step B (`election_db`): Lock the `voting_sessions` row with `FOR UPDATE`. If `session_status == 'submitted'`, immediately return the cached `confirmation_reference` without re-inserting a ballot.
   - Step C (`election_db`): Set `session_status = 'submitting'` and record `idempotency_key`.
   - Step D (`ballot_db`): Validate ballot payload structure and insert the anonymous record into `ballot_db.ballots`.
   - Step E (`election_db`): Insert the permanent participation record into `election_participation` (`voter_record_id`, `election_id`, `confirmation_reference`). The database constraint `UNIQUE(election_id, voter_record_id)` prevents any concurrent device submission. Set `voting_sessions.session_status = 'submitted'`, store `confirmation_reference`, and commit.
   - **Rollback handling:** If inserting into `election_participation` fails (e.g. concurrent submission race), the `ballot_db` insertion is rolled back or flagged invalid before commit, ensuring no orphan ballot is counted and voter status is strictly consistent.
3. **Idempotency:**
   - Retries with the same `idempotency_key` query `voting_sessions` and return the existing `confirmation_reference` immediately.
4. **Confirmation Screen:**
   - Displays green checkmark, confirmation reference (e.g. `PW-ELEC-492817`), and instructions.
   - On voting centre terminals, provides an automated 15-second countdown to reset or an immediate **"Finish & Reset Terminal"** button.

## 9. Supervised Multi-PC Voting Centres

1. **Terminal Registration & Authorization:**
   - Terminals are registered to a voting centre by institution admins in `/admin/voting-centres`.
   - Each terminal receives an authorization token hash used for heartbeats and request authentication (`POST /api/voting-terminals/{id}/heartbeat`).
   - The kiosk browser loads `/terminals/:terminalId/booth` and stores the terminal bearer token. Kiosk voter API requests pass `X-Terminal-Token` so the backend automatically binds `voting_sessions.terminal_id`.
2. **Terminal Kiosk Lifecycle & Supervisor Reset:**
   - Lifecycle: `idle` -> `identifier_entry` -> `challenge_verification` -> `voting` -> `review` -> `submitting` -> `resetting` -> `idle`.
   - The kiosk polls `POST /api/voting-terminals/{id}/heartbeat` every 5 seconds. If the supervisor triggers a reset (`POST /api/voting-terminals/{id}/reset`), the heartbeat response instructs the kiosk to wipe in-memory state and return to `idle`.
   - On completion, timeout, or reset: client wipes in-memory storage, token cookies, and drafts, and navigates to the clean idle screen. Browser back navigation is trapped to prevent viewing previous voter data.
3. **Supervisor Dashboard (`/admin/voting-centres`):**
   - Displays live terminal cards with status pills (Idle, Active Voting, Review, Offline, Last heartbeat).
   - Allows supervisors to trigger a remote terminal reset if a terminal is abandoned or idle.
   - Supervisors can never view ballot selections or individual vote contents.

## 10. API Specification Summary

### Voter Auth Config (Admin)
- `GET /api/elections/{id}/voter-auth-config`: Fetch config.
- `PUT /api/elections/{id}/voter-auth-config`: Update config (allowed before lock).
- `POST /api/elections/{id}/voter-auth-config/lock`: Freeze authentication configuration.

### Voter Roll (Admin)
- `GET /api/elections/{id}/voters`: List voters with pagination, status, and filter.
- `POST /api/elections/{id}/voters/preview`: Upload CSV, parse, return validation report.
- `POST /api/elections/{id}/voters/import`: Commit parsed CSV records.
- `POST /api/elections/{id}/voters`: Manually add individual voter record.
- `DELETE /api/elections/{id}/voters/{voter_id}`: Remove voter before voting opens.

### Voter Authentication (Public/Voter)
- `POST /api/elections/{id}/voter-auth/request-challenge`: Submit identifier, receive on-screen OTP code and challenge ID.
- `POST /api/elections/{id}/voter-auth/verify-challenge`: Submit entered OTP, receive session token.
- `POST /api/elections/{id}/voter-auth/regenerate-challenge`: Regenerate code after cooldown.
- `POST /api/elections/{id}/voter-auth/end-session`: Cancel voting session.

### Ballot & Submission (Voter Session)
- `GET /api/elections/{id}/ballot`: Fetch positions and candidates for the active session.
- `PUT /api/elections/{id}/ballot/draft`: Save progression draft.
- `POST /api/elections/{id}/ballot/submit`: Submit final ballot atomically.
- `GET /api/elections/{id}/ballot/submission-status`: Check submission state.

### Voting Centres & Terminals (Admin / Supervisor / Kiosk)
- `GET /api/voting-centres`: List centres and terminals.
- `POST /api/voting-centres`: Create centre.
- `POST /api/voting-centres/{id}/terminals`: Register terminal.
- `POST /api/voting-terminals/{id}/heartbeat`: Terminal heartbeat with status update.
- `POST /api/voting-terminals/{id}/reset`: Supervisor reset terminal.

## 11. Frontend Routes & Component Architecture

1. **`/elections/:id/vote` (Unified Voter Booth):**
   - Independent full-screen or mobile layout (free of admin sidebar).
   - Multi-phase flow:
     - Phase 1: `ElectionLanding`
     - Phase 2: `IdentifierEntry` (only the configured field)
     - Phase 3: `OTPChallengeDisplayAndVerify` (displays 6 digits prominently + input)
     - Phase 4: `BallotInstructions`
     - Phase 5: `PositionScreen` (1 position per screen, candidate cards, skip button, next/prev)
     - Phase 6: `BallotReview` (summary table, edit buttons, submit)
     - Phase 7: `SubmissionConfirmation` (receipt, auto-reset timer for kiosks)
2. **`/admin` -> "Voter Authentication & Roster" Tab:**
   - Config card: Identifier type selector, display label, OTP rules, lock status.
   - Voter roll card: Stats (Total, Eligible, Submitted, Invalid), CSV drag-and-drop, validation modal, manual add form, search/filter table.
3. **`/admin/voting-centres` (Voting Centre & Supervisor Hub):**
   - Manage centres and terminals.
   - Live supervisor monitor grid with remote reset actions.

## 12. Security, Integrity, and Auditability

- **Cryptographic Separation:** `ballot_db` contains zero foreign keys or links to `election_voter_records`.
- **Zero Plaintext OTP Storage:** Backend stores salted SHA-256 hashes of OTPs and session tokens.
- **Log Hygiene:** Plaintext OTPs, ballot choice contents, and session tokens are strictly excluded from logging statements.
- **Rate Limiting:** Protect identifier challenge requests and OTP verification endpoints against brute-force attacks.
- **Atomic Concurrency Defense:** `UNIQUE(election_id, voter_record_id)` in `election_participation` guarantees that concurrent submissions from two devices or terminals result in exactly one accepted ballot and one error.
